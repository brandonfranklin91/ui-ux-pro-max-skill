About

The person you’ll actually work with

I’m Brandon. I started this studio in 2019 after a decade of watching good strategy die somewhere between the deck and the build. So I kept the team small and the handoffs at zero — the person who draws the logotype is the person who writes the shader it ends up living inside.

That means fewer projects a year, and a lot more of me on each one. No account layer, no junior doing the parts nobody wants. If we work together, you’re working with me.
