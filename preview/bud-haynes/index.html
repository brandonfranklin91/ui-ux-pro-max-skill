Quick check

When did you last check your filter?

A filter loaded with dust makes the whole system work harder to move air through your home. Drag the handle to see the difference.

Clean Overdue

  • Look

    Slide the filter out and take a look every so often — more often in heavy heating or cooling seasons.

  • Compare

    Grey, matted, or blocking the light when you hold it up? It is likely due.

  • Replace

    Filter types and homes vary. Follow the recommendations for your system and filter — or ask us at your next visit.

New systems

Which one fits
your home?

Three common ways to heat and cool a house. The right one depends on the home itself, so we look before we recommend.

Central Air

An outdoor condenser paired with an indoor coil and a furnace or air handler. Cool air travels through the home’s ductwork to every room.

Does
Cooling, paired with a furnace for heat
Uses
Existing ductwork
Often suits
Homes already set up with ducts

Heat Pumps

One system for both seasons. A heat pump moves heat rather than making it — out of the house in summer, into it in winter.

Does
Heating and cooling
Uses
Ductwork, with an indoor air handler
Often suits
Homes wanting one system year-round

Ductless Systems

A compact outdoor unit connected to one or more wall-mounted indoor units. Each room gets its own control, and no ductwork is needed.

Does
Heating and cooling, room by room
Uses
No ductwork
Often suits
Additions, shops and rooms without ducts
